A New Dealership Built on Honesty

NextGen Auto Group was founded by Mohamad Karkoura with one goal: to make buying a used car a stress-free, honest experience for everyone in the Knoxville area.

We're a small, owner-operated dealership — which means when you come in, you deal directly with us. No big-dealership runaround, no hidden fees, no pressure.
